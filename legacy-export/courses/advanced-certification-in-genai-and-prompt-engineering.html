Generative AI Course FAQs

Short answers to common questions about the course, AI careers and what you build during training.

What is this generative AI course?

It is our 6-month, 144-hour Advanced Certification that teaches prompt engineering, Python, NLP, LLMs, LangChain, RAG and AI app deployment through hands-on projects.

What is prompt engineering?

Prompt engineering is writing clear instructions for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude so they give accurate, useful and consistent results.

Can a non-technical person join?

Yes. The course starts with AI basics and Python from zero, so beginners and non-coders can follow along.

Where is the generative AI course in Delhi held, and is there an online option?

Classroom batches run at our Greater Kailash II campus in South Delhi. You can also join the online generative AI course with the same live classes, projects and mentor support.

Is Gen AI for digital marketing covered?

Yes. A dedicated module shows how to use generative AI for content, SEO, social media, ad creatives, research and workflow automation.

What are LangChain and RAG?

LangChain is a framework for building apps on top of LLMs. RAG lets an AI answer from your own documents. This works as a hands-on LangChain and RAG course: you build both yourself.

Which tools will I learn?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, Runway, LangChain, Hugging Face, Copilot, Streamlit, Gradio and Python libraries.

Will I build projects?

Yes. You complete 10 portfolio projects, including a prompt library, a LangChain chatbot, a RAG app over your documents and a deployed Streamlit app.

Will I get a prompt engineering certification?

Yes. You earn the DizitalAdda Advanced Certification in Generative AI and Prompt Engineering, plus Skill India recognition.

How is this different from the Diploma in Gen AI?

This Advanced course is 6 months. The Diploma is 12 months and goes deeper into topics like fine-tuning and MLOps.

What jobs can I get after this course?

Prompt Engineer, AI Tools Specialist, GenAI Developer, AI Application Developer, AI Content Strategist and AI Automation Specialist.

Do you provide placement support?

Yes, 100% Placement Assistance: AI resume and GitHub portfolio review, mock AI interviews and referrals through our 250+ hiring partners.

What are the batch timings?

Morning, afternoon, evening and weekend batches are available, online and in the classroom. New batches start every 2 to 3 weeks.
