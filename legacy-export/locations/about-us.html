Who We Are

Who We Are

DizitalAdda, operated by DA DigitalAdda Innovations Pvt. Ltd., is one of India's most reviewed skill training institutes, based in Greater Kailash II, New Delhi. We specialise in career-oriented courses in Digital Marketing, Generative AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, Data Analytics, and Cyber Security & Ethical Hacking.

We were founded in 2009 with a single belief: that practical, industry-connected training — not classroom theory — is what actually gets students hired. Over the past 16 years, that belief has not changed. What has changed is the scale: we have now trained 25,000+ students and working professionals from across India and placed them at companies including Flipkart, Nykaa, Myntra, Growisto, Performics, Dentsu, and 250+ other organisations.

We operate in a corporate campus environment in South Delhi, with small batches of maximum 15 students per cohort, so every learner receives direct mentor attention rather than being lost in a crowd. Our trainers are not academic instructors — they are full-time industry professionals who manage live campaigns, real budgets, and active client accounts every day.

We are recognised by the Government of India's Skill India and Digital India programmes, and our certifications are co-branded with Google, Meta Blueprint, HubSpot, and Semrush - credentials that every recruiter in India recognises.

Our Story

Our Story — 16 Years of Building Careers

DizitalAdda was founded in 2009 by Dr. Gulshan Kumar at a time when digital marketing was still considered a niche skill in India. Dr. Kumar saw what most institutes missed: that the future of employment was going to be built on digital skills, and that students needed real-world training — not just theoretical knowledge — to compete in that future.

From a single classroom in South Delhi, the institute grew organically, driven entirely by student results and word-of-mouth. Every year, the curriculum was updated to stay ahead of industry changes: when Google launched new Ads features, when Meta's ad platform evolved, when AI tools began reshaping marketing, when data science became the backbone of business decision-making — DizitalAdda updated its programmes accordingly.

Today, the institute runs 18+ courses across four core domains — Digital Marketing, AI & Data Science, Cyber Security, and Web Development — all designed with one outcome in mind: getting students into well-paying, stable jobs within 3 to 6 months of course completion.
