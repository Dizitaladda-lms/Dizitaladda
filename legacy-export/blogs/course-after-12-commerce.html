What Are the Suitable Courses For The Students After 12th In Commerce?

For the students who have just completed their 12th in the stream of Commerce, there are so many questions but one of them is common, especially considering the organised structure of the commerce study in which “What are the best courses after 12th” targets the majority. This is a rational question for even the course you select plays a crucial role in determining your work options in the future. 

As there are different professional careers to select from, students usually face the difficulty of figuring out the best professional options. The establishment of the new economy requires new knowledge, whether you are considering traditional Economics, Finance, Accounting, Business, or such dynamic and niche economical fields as Digital Marketing, decision wise, it is timely. 

In this article, we will provide you the knowledge on the relevant and equally best courses after 12th Commerce and one such popular course would be Digital Marketing. Along with this, we shall discuss two popular and the best digital marketing institutes in Delhi from where the readers can pursue Digital Marketing Course and begin their career in Digital Space. 

Why Should One Bother about The Discipline and The Subject They Will Take Up After 12th Commerce? 

Complications arise in the case of selecting the right course after 12th commerce. Here are some of the explanations: 

  1. Career Foundation: As in every discipline, the foremost aim of the disciples in commerce is to select a course or a degree or program depending on the understanding of what that particular field has to offer when it comes to furthering a professional career, hence the first variable a course will influence is the career path and specialisation of focus a person gets.

  2. Long-Term Satisfaction: Taking the right course that you enjoy will create a profitable career for you later on. 

  3. Growth Potential: Some Industries are better than others in terms of growth. You should pick well. 

  4. Flexibility in Career: The correct course can provide the flexibility of Freelancing, Corporate or Entrepreneurship. 

While B.Com and BBA are traditional courses that were taken for the ages, students of Commerce are now beginning to embrace newer courses like Digital Marketing. Digital Marketing could be one of the best courses after 12th Commerce, let us see how. 

Why Digital Marketing is a Great Course After 12th Commerce. 

As the world becomes more and more integrated with the internet, one of the most preferred career choices is Digital Marketing for Commerce students. Here’s why: 

  1. Skill-Based Learning: Digital marketing is all about imparting practical skills to its students such as education on SEO, content marketing, social media marketing, and PPC, which easily land students jobs. 

  2. Creative and Analytical Skills: Digital marketing does involve a lot of creativity and analysis making it a perfect fit for students who wish to hold such positions.

  3. Career Flexibility: In industries as diverse as technology and retail, individuals have the option of pursuing digital marketing on a freelance basis, telecommuting, or working for large corporations.

  4. Short-Term Learning: Unlike a regular degree program or a diploma, which takes several years of schooling, a career in digital marketing is much less time consuming and offers students a quick course length of 3-6 months.

  5. Global Opportunities: Because there is a significant shortage of skilled digital marketers on a global scale, this industry allows you to work from the location of your choosing.

Top Courses After 12th Commerce

In case you are confused about what to do after 12th Commerce, there are few best courses which can support long-term, career orientated growth.

1. Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)

  • Duration: 3 years

  • Overview: The Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) is probably one of the more popular and traditional courses preferred by students from the commerce stream. It offers great subject areas in accounting, finance, economic and business laws.

  • Career Opportunities: Accountant, Finance Manager, Tax Consultant, Business Analyst.

2. Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

  • Duration: 3 years

  • Overview: A BBA is a course that is of a management perspective, there are many spheres like business management, human resources, marketing, which is related to this course.

  • Career Opportunities: Business Development Manager, HR Manager, Marketing Executive, Operations Manager.

3. Chartered Accountancy (CA)

  • Duration: 3-4 years

  • Overview: CA is a well-respected course which encompasses financial accounting, accounting, taxation, and management. This course is best-suited for learners who are keen on finance and accounts.

  • Career Opportunities: Accountant, Tax Consultant, Financial Manager, Auditor.

4. Company Secretary (CS)

  • Duration: 3 years

  • Overview: The focus of the structured identical on governance, business law and secretarial practice. This is best for learners looking towards corporate law and management.

  • Career Opportunities: Company Secretary, Corporate Consultant, Legal Officer.

5. Digital Marketing

  • Duration: 3-6 months

  • Overview: As discussed earlier, Digital Marketing is one of the best online courses after 12th grade commerce in particular to those who wish to do business on the Internet, advertising and promoting brands.

  • Career Opportunities: Digital Marketing Manager, SEO Specialist, Social Media Manager, Content Strategist, PPC Specialist.

Why Should You Consider a Digital Marketing Course After 12th Commerce?

When considering a Digital Marketing Course, here’s why it could be the best course after 12th Commerce for you:

  • Increasing Job Fetching Power: Digital marketers are in high demand now due to the increase in online businesses.

  • A Good Amount of Salary: Even the entry level digital marketers are grabbing good offers as of now and much more for seasoned professionals.

  • Remote Work Opportunities: Most of the jobs in Digital Marketing can be done online or on a freelance basis.

  • Fast Career Growth: The digital marketing field is very dynamic, enabling career advancement without much effort.

  • Variety of Career Paths: You can focus on SEO, social media, content marketing, or analytics – there is a niche for all.

Other Career Options After 12th Commerce

Digital Marketing is an interesting professional option, but you have a handful of options to look into. Some other good courses after 12th Commerce are:

  1. Hotel Management

  • Overview: This is very popular amongst students with an interest in hospitality and tourism.

  • Career Opportunities: Hotel Manager, Event Coordinator, Chef, Guest Relations Executive.

  1. Tourism Management

  • Overview: This is best suited for students who are looking to work in the travel and tourism industry.

  • Career Opportunities: Travel Agent, Tour Executive, Tourist Consultant, Event Organiser.

  1. Advertising and Branding

  • Overview: Concerned about the creative and business aspects of advertising.

  • Career Opportunities: Brand Manager, Advertising Executive, Marketing Manager.

  1. Logistics and Supply Chain Management

  • Overview: It is a new field of work which specializes in managing the distribution networks.

  • Career Opportunities: Logistics Manager, Supply Chain Manager, Warehouse Manager.
