Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions offers quick answers to common queries, guiding users through features and functionalities effortlessly.

What is digital marketing & why is it important in 2026?

Digital marketing means promoting brands through search, social media, ads, and email instead of TV or print. It's important because most buying decisions today start with an online search, so brands without a digital presence lose customers to competitors.

Is a digital marketing course worth it in 2026?

Yes, it's one of the most in-demand, low-entry skills right now. Every business needs online visibility, so trained marketers stay employable across industries.

Best digital marketing institute in Delhi with placement?

DizitalAdda offers practical, hands-on training with placement support in Delhi. Other well-known names include IIDE and Digital Vidya and DIDM, but hands-on project work matters more than brand name.

Fee of digital marketing course in Delhi?

Fees usually range from ₹25,000 to ₹1,35,000+. The exact cost depends on course level, duration, and whether placement support is included.

How many months to learn digital marketing?

Most job-ready courses take 3–6 months, while an Expert-level course with 1-year certification offers deeper, more advanced training. Basic skills can be picked up faster, but real mastery comes with hands-on practice afterward.

Best certification for digital marketing?

Google Ads, Google Analytics, and Meta Blueprint are the most recognized free certifications. Pairing these with an institute certificate that includes live project work makes a stronger profile.

Digital Marketing Course for working professionals?

Yes, most institutes including DizitalAdda run weekend or evening batches. This lets professionals learn without leaving their job.

Average salary for a digital marketer in Delhi?

Freshers typically earn ₹3.5–4.5 LPA. With 3–5 years of experience, this rises to ₹7–14 LPA.

Which skill pays most — SEO, PPC, or SMM?

PPC and SEO tend to pay the highest since both directly drive measurable ROI. Social media marketing also pays well in 2026 and is growing fast, especially for those skilled in reels, influencer marketing, and paid social ads.

Highest salary for a digital marketer in Delhi?

Senior roles like Digital Marketing Head or Performance Marketing Lead can reach ₹25–40 LPA+. This level needs 8-10+ years of experience and proven campaign results.

Can course fees be paid in installments?

Yes, most institutes including DizitalAdda offer EMI or installment options. Always confirm the number of installments and any extra charges before enrolling.

Does DizitalAdda offer online and offline courses?

Yes, both formats are available. Students can choose in-person classroom training or live online sessions based on convenience.

Yes, DizitalAdda offers a 3-year master's course in digital marketing, delivered through partnerships with selected colleges.

What is ethical hacking, and how is it different from cyber security in 2026?

Ethical hacking is the practice of legally testing systems by simulating attacks to find security weaknesses before real hackers do. Cyber security is the broader field covering all defense measures, tools, and policies used to protect systems — ethical hacking is one offensive skill within it, not the whole field.

What is the average salary of an ethical hacker in India in 2026 (fresher vs experienced)?

Freshers typically earn ₹3.5–6 LPA, especially with a CEH certification and some hands-on lab or project work. Experienced professionals (5+ years) with certifications like OSCP earn ₹15–30 LPA, and senior red-team leads can cross ₹40 LPA

Is a machine learning or AI course worth it in 2026 for a career switch?

Yes, ML/AI remains one of the highest-demand skill areas in 2026 as companies embed AI across products and operations. It suits career switchers with some logical/analytical or coding background, though expect a real learning curve if coming from a non-technical field.

Does DizitalAdda also offer Data Analytics, Data Science, and Machine Learning courses?

Yes, DizitalAdda offers Data Analytics, Data Science, and Machine Learning courses alongside its digital marketing programs. These courses include practical, project-based training with placement support, available in both online and offline formats.

What is the difference between data analytics and data science?

Data analytics focuses on examining existing data to find patterns and answer specific business questions. Data science is broader — it includes analytics plus building predictive models, machine learning, and working with unstructured data to solve more complex, forward-looking problems.

What AI tools are used in digital marketing in 2026?

Common tools include ChatGPT and Jasper for content, Surfer SEO and Semrush AI for SEO, Canva and Adobe Firefly for design, Google Performance Max and Meta Advantage+ for ad bidding, and HubSpot or Mailchimp AI for email personalization.
