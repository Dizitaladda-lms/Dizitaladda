What is LLMO and why is it taught in this digital marketing course?

LLMO stands for Large Language Model Optimisation — the practice of structuring a brand's online content so that AI models like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude recommend and cite that brand in their responses. In 2026, a significant and growing portion of search journeys begin in AI chat interfaces rather than traditional search engines. Brands that appear in AI-generated answers get discovery without clicks — which means marketers who understand LLMO, AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation), and GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) are among the most in-demand specialists in the industry.
